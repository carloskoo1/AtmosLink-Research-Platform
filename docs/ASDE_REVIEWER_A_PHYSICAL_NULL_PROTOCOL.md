# Reviewer A — prospective physical-null calibration gate

**Status:** readiness specification, not a claim of statistical PASS. Existing episode durations and operating metadata were inspected before this specification; no alternate-configuration RF/weather outcome was used to choose these criteria. These thresholds govern future analysis readiness, not a preregistered evaluation of the already observed durations. The registered 7000/20 D_validation block remains analytically reserved.
**Distinct nulls:** (i) Randomly assigned sham event times can be independent of RF *by construction*, conditional on the fixed scheduling and eligible observation times; this tests the algorithm on a physical RF background. (ii) Natural weather events are not randomly assigned. A sham rate cannot establish exchangeability or field Type-I control for natural atmospheric candidates. Document and test the additional assumptions separately; do not transfer the result automatically.

## Episode eligibility before outcome access

- Record source SHA-256 and freeze the episode start/end, link, frequency/width, AP/SM power, dual-association status, telemetry cadence and outage/maintenance log. Apply the paired-offset observation rule from `eb25a18` without revising it based on results.
- A calibration episode must have **four disjoint 72-hour blocks** at one stable configuration, at least 95% scheduled 5-min telemetry metadata coverage across the episode, and enough temporal geometry for **23** refractory atmospheric anchors separated by 360 min with two-hour edge margins. The 23-event threshold matches the smallest frozen v21 library family. These are necessary comparability/readiness gates, not proof of eight discordant RF events or p-value validity. Do not concatenate configurations to manufacture one episode.
- Before accessing outcomes, an RF-blinded field log must adjudicate maintenance, outages, configuration transitions and whether any proposed *natural* negative-control exposure is plausibly null. Stable power/association alone does not make meteorology causally null. Random sham anchors require no natural-weather null label, but test a different question.
- For a design-based sham diagnostic, prespecify four independent sham schedules with 23/28/28/28 events and >=360-min spacing in each eligible episode, drawn from eligible times without consulting RF values. Freeze RNG seed, calendar stratification, minimum paired offsets and the entire 4×3 test family. Evaluate empirical library-wide selection across randomized sham schedules **within** an episode, reporting conditional randomization variation. Distinct draws on one RF series are not independent physical episodes.
- Use separate physical episode/link units for uncertainty about future operation. Report the number of independent units, not the number of sham draws, and interval estimates clustered by episode. A zero-selection one-sided 95% upper bound falls below 5% only after at least **59 independent null episodes**; this arithmetic illustrates why a few short configurations cannot certify a field-wide 5% guarantee. The weather-null problem also requires defensible exchangeability/negative-control assumptions beyond this count.
- If any readiness or independent-null condition fails, label the study `PILOT / NOT CALIBRATED`. Do not inspect D_validation to choose the null or tune event rules. An AI pilot cannot repair this statistical gate.

**Primary statistical caution:** Romano and Tirlea, “Permutation testing for dependence in time series,” *Journal of Time Series Analysis* 43(5):781–807 (2022), DOI [10.1111/jtsa.12638](https://doi.org/10.1111/jtsa.12638): exact permutation levels require exchangeability; dependence/null changes can invalidate unmodified permutation tests. The above sham design is a proposed ASDE diagnostic, not a theorem about natural weather–RF independence.
