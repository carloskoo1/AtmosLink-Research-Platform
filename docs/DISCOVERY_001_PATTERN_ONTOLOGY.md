# DISCOVERY-001 Pattern Ontology

The discovery engine must distinguish pattern classes before scientific interpretation.

## PRECURSOR_CANDIDATE
A temporally asymmetric pattern that occurs before an RF event, survives blocked development robustness, and does not remain equivalently present after the event. This class may advance toward a frozen hypothesis only after all registered gates pass.

## CONTEXT_MARKER
A pattern reproducibly associated with RF events but present both before and after them. It may characterize an environmental or operational regime, but it is not evidence of temporal precedence or causation.

## CONSEQUENCE_OR_RECOVERY
A pattern primarily detectable after an RF event. It may describe system response, recovery, or a downstream process, but not a precursor.

## SCREENED_OUT
A candidate that fails replication, QC eligibility, temporal independence, strict directionality, multiplicity control, or another registered scientific gate.

## EVIDENCE_ACCUMULATING
A candidate that preserves a prespecified effect across development checks but lacks sufficient independent events or precision. This status does not authorize access to D_validation.

## HYPOTHESIS / FROZEN
A candidate becomes a hypothesis only after human review. It becomes FROZEN only after its definition, features, temporal window, outcome, QC rules, statistical test, multiplicity treatment and decision rule are fixed before validation.
